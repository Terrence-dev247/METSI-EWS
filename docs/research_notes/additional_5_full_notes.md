## INC-2023-107 — Power failures at multiple Rand Water plants
**Date:** 2023-06-26 | **Source:** https://www.tshwane.gov.za/?p=58155
**Failure node(s):** Vereeniging & Zuikerbosch WTPs, Palmiet/Eikenhof/Mapleton Booster Pumping Stations — all outside Tshwane.

> The City of Tshwane has been notified by Rand Water about inconsistent supply challenges that are underway, affecting Tshwane and other municipalities. This follows a number of power failures and trips experienced during June 2023 at the Vereeniging and Zuikerbosch Water Treatment Plants as well as at the Palmiet, Eikenhof and Mapleton Booster Pumping Stations.
>
> Due to these power failures, the entire water utility system has felt a knock-on effect of unstable supply. This resulted in the depletion of water at the following Rand Water reservoirs: Brakpan, Bronberge, Hartebeeshoek, Klipfontein, Selcourt, Stompiesfontein, Vlakfontein, Wildebeesfontein.
>
> Rand Water has confirmed that some areas within municipalities are likely to experience low water pressure to no water during the recovery of its system. This is now evident in the case of the areas north of Pretoria, where there is low water pressure to no water in places like Soshanguve, Mabopane and Ga-Rankuwa. The Rand Water reservoirs supplying the City of Tshwane are Vlakfontein, Klipfontein, Hartebeeshoek, and Bronberge.
>
> [Standard water-saving appeal follows.]

## INC-2023-108 — Power trip at Mapleton Booster Pumping Station
**Date:** 2023-07-14 | **Source:** https://www.tshwane.gov.za/?p=59216
**Failure node:** Mapleton Booster Pumping Station, Boksburg — outside Tshwane (confirmed via earlier geocoding).

> The water utility, Rand Water, has informed the City of Tshwane that an additional power trip occurred last night, 13 July 2023, at Mapleton Booster Pumping Station affecting water pumping capacity at Engine Room 1. The Mapleton Booster Pumping Station pumps water that supplies Rand Water reservoirs in Pretoria East. According to the utility, technicians are on-site busy with fault finding and therefore the estimated time of restoration is not yet available.
>
> The power interruption has resulted in the depletion of the following Rand Water reservoirs: Brakpan 1 and 3, Bronberge, Selcourt, Stompiesfontein, Vlakfontein 1 and 2, Wildebeesfontein 1, 2 and 3.
>
> [Long list of affected Tshwane reservoirs/suburbs follows — Carina Street, Corobrick Plant, Garsfontein and its downstream feeds (Eersterust, Elardus Park, Kilner Park, Klapperkop, Koedoesnek LL, Magalieskruin, Mamelodi R1/R2, Montana, Moreleta, Murrayfield, Parkmore HL/LL, Queenswood, Sinoville HL/LL, Villieria Peak Tanks, Waverley HL/LL, Gastonbury/Six Fountains/Silver Willows, Mooikloof, plus several meters.]
>
> The City apologises for the inconvenience caused to all affected consumers as a result of this interruption.

## INC-2023-110 — Power dip at Zuikerbosch Water Treatment Plant
**Date:** 2023-08-25 | **Source:** https://www.tshwane.gov.za/?p=59744
**Failure node:** Zuikerbosch WTP, Vereeniging — outside Tshwane.

> Media alert, 25 August 2023.
> The City of Tshwane is aware of certain areas within the metropolitan municipality experiencing low water pressure to no water at all as of today, 25 August 2023. The water shortages are a result of Rand Water's Zuikerbosch Water Treatment Plant having suffered a power dip that resulted in the operational failure of the plant, which simply means that it could not pump.
>
> The power dip resulted in the depletion of the following Rand Water systems: Zwartkopjes System, Eikenhof System, Mapleton System, Palmiet System.
>
> Residents are assured that Eskom technicians have since addressed the voltage dip and that all Tshwane reservoir water levels are expected to normalise soon.
> Issued by Communication, Marketing and Events.

## INC-2023-111 — Zuikerbosch WTP power trip (severe thunderstorm)
**Date:** 2023-09-21 | **Source:** https://www.tshwane.gov.za/?p=60821
**Failure node:** Zuikerbosch WTP, Vereeniging — outside Tshwane. (Weather-triggered: overhead power line faults from a thunderstorm on 19 Sept.)

> On Thursday, 21 September 2023, Rand Water informed the City of Tshwane about their Zuikerbosch Water Treatment Plant that had experienced a power trip which affected production. This follows disruptions in operations due to a severe thunderstorm that occurred around midnight on Tuesday, 19 September 2023. Attempts to restart the plant revealed faults along the overhead power lines supplying the plant.
>
> The power failure resulted in the loss in production of 2 000 million litres of water per day. This load reduction impacted all municipalities supplied by Zuikerbosch Water Treatment Plant, namely the City of Tshwane, City of Ekurhuleni and City of Johannesburg, among others.
>
> Affected systems: Palmiet System (Klipriviersberg, Klipfontein, Brakfontein, Germiston, Northridge, Hartebeeshoek, Vlakfontein), Mapleton System (Brakpan, Vlakfontein, Selcourt, Wildebeestfontein, Stompiesfontein, Bronberg), Eikenhof System (Meredale, Waterval).
>
> The Mapleton System (eastern Tshwane) has since recovered and is pumping at full load. The Palmiet System (western Tshwane) has been unstable, worsened by this morning's power failure. Most reservoirs on the Palmiet Pumping System have been isolated due to critically low levels — meaning Tshwane reservoirs are currently receiving no flow from Rand Water at all.
>
> [Long list of critically-low Tshwane reservoirs follows: Akasia, Atteridgeville HL, Bakenkop, Blair Athol, Brakfontein, Carina Street, Corobrick Plant, Erasmia, Hatherley, Klapperkop, Kosmosdal, Laudium, Lotus Gardens, Louwlardia, Mabopane, Magaliesberg, Mnandi, Raslouw, Rooihuiskraal, Saulsville, Soshanguve DD/L, Sunderland Ridge, Wonderboom.]
>
> [Standard water-saving tips and incident-reporting phone numbers follow.]

## INC-2024-108 — Palmiet Booster Pump Station power trip
**Date:** 2024-11-07 | **Source:** https://www.tshwane.gov.za/?p=80487
**Failure node:** Palmiet Booster Pump Station, Alberton — outside Tshwane (confirmed via earlier geocoding).

> The City of Tshwane has been notified by Rand Water that its Palmiet Booster Pump Station is now pumping at full capacity following yesterday's power trip that affected operations at the pump station.
>
> The trip resulted in the depletion of the water utility's Klipriviersberg, Klipfontein, Brakfontein and Hartebeesthoek Reservoirs that supply some of the City's reservoirs. According to Rand Water, the affected reservoirs' levels have shown positive signs of improvement since pumping was optimised. This means that the City's reservoirs will gradually recover.
>
> Some of the City's reservoirs that are supplied by the water utility's reservoirs are sitting at extremely low levels or with no water at all. The following areas have been severely affected: Atteridgeville, Laudium, Lotus Gardens, Olievenhoutbosch, parts of Mabopane, parts of Soshanguve, Thatchfield.
>
> Customers are urged to exercise patience during the pumping period as the network takes time to fully recover. Residents will be kept updated as and when there are new developments.

## Pattern summary

Every single `power_failure`-category incident in the dataset traces back to
a Rand Water plant or pumping station physically located outside Tshwane
(Zuikerbosch/Vereeniging, Eikenhof, Palmiet/Alberton, Mapleton/Boksburg).
This isn't a coincidence specific to these 5 rows — it suggests the
`power_failure` category as a whole may represent *exogenous bulk-supply
shocks* rather than Tshwane infrastructure failures, same logic already
applied to INC-2023-101 and INC-2023-103.
